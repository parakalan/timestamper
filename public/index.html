⌨️ Shortcuts & tricks

  • Enter New timestamped entry
  • Shift+Enter Plain new line (no timestamp)
  • Alt+N New thread
  • Alt+T Cycle theme
  • Alt+F Focus mode on current thread
  • / Search
  • Esc Close things
  • ? This help

🎉 Secret sauce

  • End an entry with #win, ✅ or done! for confetti.
  • Click a thread's emoji to change it; click the dot to change its color.
  • Drag threads by their emoji to reorder. Pin the important ones 📌.
  • Finish a 🍅 pomodoro and it gets logged in your active thread.
  • Write every day to keep the 🔥 streak alive and level up.
